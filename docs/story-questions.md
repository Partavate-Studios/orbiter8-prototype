# Orbiter 8: story questions for Koda

Every open story question in one place (2026-11-19), to answer in one sitting. Gathered from
[`backstory.md`](backstory.md) ("Conflicts and calls" and "Open story questions"), the two new files from
Koda's chat of 2026-11-18 (`STORY_UPDATE_INTRO.md` and `PROJECTS_INTRO.md`, now in `~/Downloads/`), and
[`ui-guide.md`](ui-guide.md). Answer by number; each answer then goes into `backstory.md` (the story's one

Where a suggestion exists it's marked **Suggested**. Questions the new intro already settles are listed
at the end, to confirm rather than answer.

## A. The new intro (from `STORY_UPDATE_INTRO.md` §5 and §7)

**Q1. The "government check" in a world without governments.** The intro's character lives on a basic
income, but the lore says governments failed.
- (a) Rename it: a Minerva stipend, or a corporate dividend for non-citizens.
- (b) The character is a **non-citizen**, on a minimal Minerva-administered stipend; the job offer is their
  first chance at citizenship. **Suggested** (it makes choosing a corporation mean something).
- (c) Remnant governments still exist on Earth, broke and powerless.

**Q2. The Federation.** Related to Q1. The game has a Federation that holds Sol's worlds (D49) and owns
its stations and the belt; `backstory.md` keeps the old *United Solar System Federation* as Sol's
trustee, governing nothing. Keep it, rename it, or make Sol **Minerva's trust**? If Q1 is (c), the
Federation could be the remnant government.
- Also: the title screen's pilot card says "United Federation of Planets", which is Star Trek's name
  (carried over from Demo 3). Change it to whatever Q2 settles?

**Q3. Who enforces the curfew,** with no government? **Suggested:** whichever corporation owns the
district or building: a place to plant a clue about one of the three.

**Q4. The health score:** a Minerva public-health metric, or a corporate or insurer score that Minerva
only relays? What does it affect (insurance, eligibility)?

**Q5. Sponsorship or employment.** The design chats had existing players sponsor new minds; the intro has
a corporation hire you. Is sponsorship gone, or kept for later as a referral ("recommended by") mechanic?

**Q6. Can a new player start as a pirate,** choosing none of the three? **Suggested:** no; pirates have
renounced a citizenship, which you need to have had first.

**Q7. Getting your body back from cold storage:** a real endgame goal (with a price in the game), or
flavor?

**Q8. Which ship.** Does every new pilot get an Orbiter 8, or does each corporation issue its own model?
This ties to Q22 (the game's name against the ship's name).

**Q9. The year.** Is the intro set in 2140, the year of the Orbiter 8's interstellar success?

**Q10. The three corporations.** They need names, natures, industries, ad voices, job listings, histories
and what citizenship means in play. **Suggested** axes, so the choice matters: an orderly, law-heavy one
(safety), a profit-first trading one (wealth, lighter rules, higher taxes), and an expansionist frontier
one (freedom, fewer protections). Agree with the axes, and do you want to name them, or should a draft
come to you?

**Q11. The AMI on your ship:** a named companion, or a generic system?

**Q12. Minerva's voice** in messages: neutral, bureaucratic, quietly kind? A voice guide follows from the
answer, and Minerva delivers much of the game.

**Q13. Processing:** what happens between the escort and waking in orbit (the cold-storage facility, the
upload). Shown, told in Minerva's library, or left unsaid?

**Q22. The game's name against the ship's name,** if other ship lines arrive (and see Q8).

**Q23. When did the governments fail,** relative to the Great Regrettion and Minerva's founding? (Q1 and

**Q24. Who built Minerva,** and why does she predate the corporations?

**Q25. What happens to a mind whose last ship is destroyed,** and what's the in-world cost? (Ships aren't

**Q28. The founding minds:** how did the first pilots come to be, before anyone could sponsor or hire them?
(The intro's employment answer may cover it: the first volunteers.)

## Settled by the new intro (confirm, then they go into `backstory.md`)

- **Minds live on Minerva** (was Leaning, `backstory.md` conflict 5): now Decided. Pilots are uploaded;
  the body goes into cold storage and can be bought back.
- **Why minds pilot** (was conflict 2, colonists in the hold against bodies that can't make the trip):
  the intro's reason is **speed**, not survival. AMIs aren't good enough, and humans working with AMIs are
  too slow; an uploaded mind with AMI help is fast enough. So bodies *can* make the trip, and colonists
  riding a hundred to a hold no longer conflicts. Agree?
- **The tone:** Earth is the dystopia, the frontier is the hope; "Liberta Galactica!" means escape.
- **No launch:** you wake in a ship already orbiting Earth, with a message from Minerva.
- **Your corporation is your citizenship**, chosen by which of the three listings you apply to.
